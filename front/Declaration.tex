\begin{flushleft}
	{
	\noindent I, \myName, hereby declare that this thesis has not been, and will not be, submitted in whole or in part to
	another university for the award of any other degree. The work presented in this thesis includes research
	carried out in collaboration with Andrea Banfi, Melissa van Beekveld, Silvia Ferrario Ravasio, Alexander Karlberg, 
	Matthew Lim, and Daniel Reichelt.
		
	The content of chapter~\ref{chapter:DIS} is taken nearly verbatim from the paper~\cite{vanBeekveld:2026rez},
	currently under peer review:
	
	M.~van Beekveld, S.~Ferrario Ravasio, A.~Karlberg and D.~Peake,
	\textit{A generalised-$k_t$ jet algorithm for Deep Inelastic Scattering},
	arXiv:2606.13077.
	
	This algorithm builds on the angular-ordered ($p=0$) variant introduced by
	M.~van Beekveld and S.~Ferrario Ravasio in Ref.~\cite{vanBeekveld:2023chs},
	while the generalisation to parameter $p$ was developed jointly by all
	authors. I implemented the initial versions of the algorithm, which were subsequently 
	refined in collaboration with my co-authors, with M.~van Beekveld preparing the version released in the
	plugin. I performed the majority of the computational work underlying the
	phenomenological results. The phenomenological studies, comparisons, and interpretation of the results
	were developed collectively. The plotting scripts were produced
	by S.~Ferrario Ravasio using the results of my simulations, and the paper
	was written jointly by all authors.
	
	Appendix~\ref{ap:DIS-fastjet} contains
	relevant parts of the code taken directly from the
	\href{https://repo.hepforge.org/source/fastjetsvn/browse/contrib/contribs/DISGenkt}
	{\texttt{DISGenkt}} plugin.
	
	The content of chapter~\ref{ch:hhZjet} is based on a project that will be
	submitted for publication in due course:
	
	D.~Peake, A.~Banfi, M.A.~Lim and D.~Reichelt, \textit{NNLL resummation in
	$Z+$jet events with the ARES method}.
	
	I performed the majority of the calculations presented in this chapter,
	with further input from A.~Banfi, M.A.~Lim and D.~Reichelt. I calculated the NNLL
	corrections to the observable and carried out all validations
	presented here. Together with A.~Banfi, I developed the treatment of
	initial-state radiation within the ARES formalism.
		\par}
\end{flushleft}
