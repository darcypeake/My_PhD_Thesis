\chapter{One-loop hard function}
\label{ap:H1-extraction}

In this appendix, we perform the calculation required to identify
the hard coefficient $H^{(1)}_{ij}$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:H1-final} and the
one-loop hard function $C^{(1)}_H$ defined in Eq.~\eqref{eq:CH-SCET}.
These contributions rely on the one-loop virtual corrections, which for the $hh\to Z+$ jet
process are not directly available in the literature and so we use the work from Ref.~\cite{Ravindran:2002dc} which
presents the one-loop virtual amplitude for the $hh\to H+$ jet process. \ABquestion{How do we justify this is OK?}
We proceed by finding these contributions for the $q\bar{q}\to Hg$ channel where the other channels follow analogously. 

\paragraph{Pole comparison.} Expanding Eq.~\eqref{eq:HQ-expanded} in $\varepsilon$ for the
$q\bar{q}\to Zg$ process, the double-pole
coefficient is
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:H-pole2}
  -\frac{1}{\varepsilon^2}(C_A+2C_F)\,,
\end{equation}
and the single-pole coefficient is
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:H-pole1}
  \frac{1}{\varepsilon}\!\left(
  -\frac{11}{6}C_A-3C_F+\frac{n_f}{3}
  +(C_A-2C_F)\ln\frac{\mu_R^2}{Q_{12}^2}
  -C_A\ln\frac{\mu_R^2}{Q_{13}^2}
  -C_A\ln\frac{\mu_R^2}{Q_{23}^2}
  \right)\,.
\end{equation}
Comparing with the poles found in Ref.~\cite{Ravindran:2002dc}. The bare cross section at leading-order is
\begin{equation}
  Q_{12}^4\frac{d^2\sigma_{q\bar{q}\to g H}^{(1)}}{dQ_{13}^2\,dQ_{23}^2} =
  \pi\delta(Q_{12}^2-Q_{13}^2-Q_{23}^2-M^2)\,
  \frac{S_\varepsilon}{\Gamma(1-\varepsilon)}
  \,\frac{1}{4N_c^2}
  \left(\frac{\mu_0^2\,Q_{12}^2}{Q_{13}^2Q_{23}^2}\right)^\varepsilon
 \left( \frac{\alpha_s(\mu_0^2)}{4\pi}\right)^3|M_{q\bar{q}\to g H}^{(1)}|^2
  \,,
\end{equation}
where $S_\varepsilon=\exp(\varepsilon(\ln4\pi-\gamma_E))$ and $\mu_0$ is the bare reference scale. \ABquestion{I have substituted in
$G$ directly as $\alpha_s/4\pi$ but not quite sure if this is OK? The reference does say that $G^2\simeq \alpha_s(\mu_r)/4\pi$ where
$\mu_r$ is the renormalisation scale so I think this could be a mistake?}

We must renormalise the strong coupling constant due to UV divergences using,
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:Catani-renorm}
  \alpha_s(\mu_0^2) =
\alpha_s(\mu_R^2)
  \!\left[1-\frac{\alpha_s(\mu_R^2)\beta_0}{\varepsilon}
  +\mathcal{O}(\alpha_s^2)\right] \left(\frac{\mu_R^2}{\mu_0^2}\right)^\varepsilon\,,
\end{equation}
leading to the renormalised form of the LO cross-section
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
  \label{eq:LO-ren}
  Q_{12}^4\frac{d^2\sigma_{q\bar{q}\to gH}^{{\rm LO},\rm Ren}}{dQ_{13}^2\,dQ_{23}^2}& \simeq
  \pi\delta(Q_{12}^2-Q_{13}^2-Q_{23}^2-M^2)\,
  \frac{S_\varepsilon}{\Gamma(1-\varepsilon)}
  \,\frac{1}{4N_c^2}
  \left(\frac{\mu_R^2\,Q_{12}^2}{Q_{13}^2Q_{23}^2}\right)^\varepsilon
\left(\frac{\alpha_s(\mu_R^2)}{4\pi}\right)^3
\left(\frac{\mu_R^2}{\mu_0^2}\right)^{2\varepsilon}\\
&\times  \!\left[1-\frac{3\alpha_s(\mu_R^2)\beta_0}{\varepsilon}\right] |M_{q\bar{q}\to gH}^{(0)}|^2\,.
  \end{aligned}
  \end{equation}
 
The bare one-loop virtual correction have the form
\begin{equation}

 \left(\frac{\alpha_s(\mu_0^2)}{4\pi}\right)^2
   \left(\frac{\alpha_s(\mu_R^2)}{4\pi}\right)^2  \\[0.5ex]
